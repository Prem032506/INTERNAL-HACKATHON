End-to-End Operational Disruption & Rerouting Workflow

How the platform senses, assesses, resolves, and tracks logistics disruption events across NER corridors.

🌧️
Step 1: Disruption Sensed & Forecasted

Heavy rainfall threshold (>85mm/day) breached in West Kameng or Pagala Pahar. Satellite radar and IoT rain gauges trigger early hazard alert.

🧠
Step 2: AI Hazard Classification & Impact Zone Calculation

XGBoost evaluates slope gradient, soil moisture, and historical inventory, determining a 88% Landslide Vulnerability on NH-29.

🧭
Step 3: Constrained Dynamic Multimodal Rerouting

Terrain-aware optimizer generates alternate bypass via Bokajan-Wokha corridor (NH-61), verifying bridge weight limit (>30T) for cryogenic medical tankers.

📡
Step 4: Automated Dispatch & Driver Notification

Driver in-cab navigation recalculates ETA. Multilingual voice alert sent in Assamese/Nagamese. BRO Task Force and Assam Rifles checkpost notified.

✅
Step 5: Delivery Fulfilled & Ground Verification

Supply reaches destination civil hospital without cold-chain breach. BRO uploads clearance photo via offline PWA upon road restoration.

Offline-First Zero-Connectivity Synchronization Protocol

Remote Mountain Shadow Zone

1. BRO official or driver captures geo-tagged photo & incident status in remote gorge.
2. PWA Service Worker intercepts request.
3. Data serialized into IndexedDB local cache.
4. User receives instant on-device receipt.

Connectivity Resumption

1. Vehicle enters cell range or checkpost satellite WiFi.
2. Window online event triggers Sync Queue.
3. Idempotent batch upload to Central Cloud with timestamp resolution.
4. Central GIS layers update in real time across the nation.
