1. Executive Summary

The North Eastern Region (NER) of India represents over 262,000 km² of rugged mountain terrain, deep river basins, and dense subtropical forests. The entire region connects to mainland India through the narrow 22 km Siliguri Corridor ("Chicken's Neck"). During annual monsoons, intense precipitation causes severe landslides, mudflows, and river overwash along arterial highways such as NH-29 (Dimapur-Kohima-Imphal), NH-10 (Siliguri-Gangtok), and NH-13 (Trans-Arunachal Highway).

NER-Setu solves this critical challenge by providing an AI-driven, real-time logistics intelligence and accessibility management platform. By fusing satellite Earth observation, Doppler weather telemetry, IoT vehicle feeds, and offline-capable field reporting, NER-Setu forecasts road disruptions 24 to 48 hours in advance, calculates resilient alternate mountain corridors, and guarantees end-to-end visibility for essential life-saving commodities (vaccines, cryogenic oxygen, rations, petroleum).

2. Mathematical Formulation & AI Models

The probability of slope failure at any road segment is computed using the multi-parameter Hazard Vulnerability Index (HVI):

HVI = 0.35 * (Rainfall_72h / 85mm) + 0.30 * (SlopeAngle / 90°) + 0.20 * SoilMoistureSat + 0.15 * HistoricalFreq

The Dynamic Routing Engine utilizes a constrained $A^*$ search algorithm with terrain gradient penalties, bridge weight ceilings, and flood submergence avoidance:

EdgeCost(e) = Distance(e) * (1 + 0.4 * SlopeFactor(e)) + 0.5 * HVI(e) + BridgeWeightPenalty(e, VehicleMass)

3. Offline-First PWA Synchronization Protocol

Due to frequent cellular blackouts in remote mountain gorges, the field inspection module operates on an Offline-First architecture using Service Workers and local IndexedDB storage. When field officials or convoy drivers log geo-tagged incidents with photographs without network connectivity:

  • Images are compressed locally into WebP format (< 150KB).
  • Reports are serialized and stored in on-device encrypted storage with UTC timestamps and hardware GPS coordinates.
  • Upon re-entering cellular range or connecting to checkpost satellite WiFi, the Background Sync API transmits queued batches idempotently to the central cloud.
