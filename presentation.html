The Global Mountain & Disaster Supply Chain Crisis

Difficult terrain, extreme climate events, and fragile single-road corridors disrupt essential humanitarian relief.

Vulnerable Arterial Chokepoints

From India's Siliguri Corridor & NH-29 to Europe's Gotthard Pass and the Andean Paso Los Libertadores, a single landslide or blizzard severs critical supply arteries.
